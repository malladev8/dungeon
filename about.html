About

Hail and well met! Thank you for considering me as your Game Master for your next adventure.

My Story

My name is Eric, and I've been playing Dungeons & Dragons for nearly a decade. I first discovered the game around a table with friends, gathering in a hot garage to slay monsters, attempt questionable accents, and embark on unforgettable adventures. Before long, I found myself drawn behind the screen, eager to build worlds and tell stories of my own.

Since becoming a Game Master in 2019, I've run weekly campaigns for groups of friends, including a six-year homebrew campaign that reached level 20 and a complete playthrough of Out of the Abyss. I've also run adventures from Curse of Strahd, Call of the Netherdeep, and Rime of the Frostmaiden, along with numerous homebrew one-shots for both veteran and first-time players.

My Approach

Every group is different, so I strive to build campaigns around the players at my table. I enjoy creating immersive worlds where your choices matter, your characters have room to grow, and every session feels like another chapter in your own story. My background as a software engineer has also shaped how I design encounters and adventures. I enjoy creating challenges that reward teamwork, creativity, and clever problem solving while ensuring each player has opportunities to contribute.

The "Rule of Cool" and the rules as written don't have to be enemies. I believe the rules provide a fair foundation for everyone at the table, but I'm always willing to reward creative ideas when they make the game more exciting and memorable.

I believe consistency is one of the most important qualities a Game Master can have. When my players set aside an evening for adventure, they can count on me to be prepared and ready to deliver a memorable session.

Above all else, D&D is a game. It should be fun!
