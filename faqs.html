FAQs

What if I've never played D&D before?

No problem! I enjoy introducing new players and will guide you through character creation and the rules.

What do I need to play?

Varies depending on campaign and session format. See the Adventures page for more information.

How long are sessions?

Sessions typically last between 2-4 hours, but this can vary based on the campaign and group preferences.

What if I have to miss a session?

If you need to miss a session, please notify me as soon as possible. Depending on the circumstances, we may be able to reschedule or find a solution.

What virtual tabletop platforms do you use?

For online sessions, we use Discord for voice and video and dndbeyond for character sheets and dice rolls. Maps will be built physically and streamed over discord.

Are your games family-friendly?

Yes! I strive to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for players of all ages. Content will be appropriate for the group, and I can adjust themes and language as needed.

Do you allow homebrew content?

Sometimes. If you are interested in creating your own character content, please discuss it with me beforehand and I will do my best to accommodate it.
