FAQs

What if I've never played D&D before?

No problem! I enjoy introducing new players and will guide you through character creation and the rules. Check out my New Player Experience!

How many people can play in a session?

The recommended number of players for D&D 5th Edition is between 3 and 5, but this can vary based on the campaign and group preferences.

What do I need to play?

This varies depending on game format. See the Services page for more information.

How long are D&D sessions?

Campaign sessions typically last between 3-4 hours, but this can vary based on the campaign and group preferences. One-Shots typically run 3-5 hours in order to fully conclude the adventure.

How are payments handled?

See the Campaign or One-Shot Pricing sections on the Services page for information on payments. Payment links will be provided in the confirmation email.

What if I have to miss a Campaign session?

See the Campaign Pricing section on the Services page for information on cancellations and refunds. Don't worry, I'll try not to kill your character while you're away!

What virtual tabletop platforms do you use?

For online sessions, we use Discord for voice and video and dndbeyond for character sheets and dice rolls. Maps will be built physically and streamed over discord.

Are your games family-friendly?

Yes! I strive to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for players of all ages. Content will be appropriate for the group, and I can adjust themes and language as needed.

Do you allow homebrew content?

Sometimes. If you are interested in creating your own character content, please discuss it with me beforehand and I will do my best to accommodate it.
