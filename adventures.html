Game Formats

I offer online and in-person game options.

Online

Discord will be used for voice and video commication.
Dndbeyond will be used for character sheets and dice rolls.
Maps will be built physically and streamed over Discord.

In-Person

I can accommodate in-person games in the greater Chicago area. Please contact me for more information.
In-person players have the option to use dndbeyond or physical character sheets and dice.
I can provide physical character sheets and loaner dice.
I will provide physical maps, miniatures, and everything else needed to run the game.

Book as a Group or Individual

Whether you've got your full party assembled or are looking to team up with other would-be heroes from across the realm, I've got you covered.
If you're looking for a group to join, I'll do my best to match you with the right party based on your preferences and experience level.
