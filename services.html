Campaigns

Multi-session adventures where your characters, narratives, and relationships can develop over time.

Every campaign includes a complimentary Session 0 where we'll discuss expectations, create characters together, establish table rules, and make sure everyone is excited for the adventure ahead.

Original Campaigns

Original campaigns are custom adventures built specifically for your group. Whether you choose an established setting or my own world of Xhaula, your decisions will shape the story from beginning to end.

Published Campaigns

Published Campaigns are pre-written stories that have been officially released by Wizards of the Coast or other publishers.
Published Campaigns provide a carefully crafted story while still allowing plenty of room for player choice, roleplaying, and memorable moments unique to your group.
