Campaigns

Campaigns are multi-session story arcs that can be played as a group or joined individually.
Campaigns are ideal for players looking to experience an ongoing story, develop their characters over time, and build lasting friendships around the table.

Every campaign includes a complimentary Session 0 where we'll discuss expectations, create characters together, establish table rules, and ensure everyone is excited for the adventure ahead.

Original Campaigns

Original campaigns are custom adventures built specifically for your group. Whether you choose an established setting or my own world of Xhaula, your decisions will shape the story from beginning to end.

Faerûn (The Forgotten Realms)

Explore D&D's most iconic setting, where bustling cities, ancient ruins, legendary factions, and countless adventures await. Whether you seek political intrigue, epic quests, or classic high fantasy, Faerûn offers endless possibilities.

Exandria

Journey across the world made famous by Critical Role, where powerful nations, forgotten gods, and ancient magic shape a rich and evolving world. Forge your own legend in a setting filled with memorable characters and limitless adventure.

Theros

Step into a world inspired by Greek mythology, where mighty heroes, terrifying monsters, and the will of the gods define every tale. Earn glory, challenge fate, and carve your name into legend in this epic fantasy setting.

Xhaula

Embark on a truly unique adventure in my custom high fantasy world, where your choices don't just shape the story, they become part of the world's history.

Published Adventures

Published adventures are pre-written stories that have been officially released by Wizards of the Coast or other publishers.
Published adventures provide a carefully crafted story while still allowing plenty of room for player choice, roleplaying, and memorable moments unique to your group.
